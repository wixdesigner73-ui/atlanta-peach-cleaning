Our Story

Professional Cleaning, Done Dependably

Atlanta Peach Cleaning Company, LLC provides professional cleaning services for residential and commercial spaces. From the home you return to every evening to the workplace your team relies on every day, we help keep spaces clean, fresh, and welcoming.

Under the leadership of Ms. Cheryl Kates, our focus is on dependable, professional service: showing up, paying attention to the details, and treating every space with care.
